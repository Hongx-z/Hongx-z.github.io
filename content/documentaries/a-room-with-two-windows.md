Chidera has been in this flat for two years, which is the longest she has stayed anywhere since she was nineteen. The room has two windows, which is why she took it, and which is the only reason this film is watchable — the light changes four times over the twelve minutes.

The film is about a box. She has carried it through four countries. She has not opened it since the second one.

## Director's note

This is the shortest film in the series and it took the longest to get permission for, because the whole thing is about objects and objects are more exposing than faces.

I asked her, at the beginning, what she did not want filmed. She said the box. So for the first three days the box was in every frame and I did not touch it, which is a specific kind of discipline that I recommend to anyone making a portrait of a room.

On the fourth day she opened it, and told me to keep filming. What is in it is in the film. I am not going to describe it here, because the film is twelve minutes long and the description would take about ten seconds and would be worse.

## After

This was the first film in the series with no music at all. The decision was made in the mix and it is the only one of these decisions I have never second-guessed.
