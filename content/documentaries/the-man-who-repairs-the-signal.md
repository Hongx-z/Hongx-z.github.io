Viktor is fifty-one and has been doing this route for nine years. Eleven stations, one van, about four hundred kilometres of mountain road a week. When a relay fails, a valley loses its signal — mobile, radio, and in two cases the emergency service's own backhaul.

He describes the job as "driving with extra steps". He is being modest in the specific way that people are modest when the thing they do is genuinely unusual.

## Director's note

Twenty-six minutes is long for this series and I have been asked twice why it is not fourteen. The answer is that I could not cut the driving, and the driving is the film.

Viktor talks while he drives. Not constantly — there are silences of four and five minutes. I kept every one of them, against the advice of everybody who watched the assembly, because they are the reason the last eight minutes work. By the time he gets to the eleventh station, you have spent twenty minutes in a van with him, and you are ready to hear what he says there.

## What he asked me to keep

One thing. At the end of the third day he told me about a winter when he was snowed in at station seven for two nights, and he asked me, unprompted, to leave that in. It is the only time in four years a subject has asked me to include something rather than remove it.

It is in.
