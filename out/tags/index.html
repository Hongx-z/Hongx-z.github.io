  1. Fatima

    The Last Shift

    A film about Fatima·18 min·Nov 2025

    Fatima ran the same extrusion line for nineteen years. On the plant's final night she enters the word "decommissioned" into a terminal for the first time in her life, and then goes home.

  2. Idris

    The Water Reader

    A film about Idris·22 min·Jul 2025

    Idris can tell the level of an aquifer by the sound a pump makes. He has been doing it for thirty years, the instruments that were supposed to replace him keep failing, and nobody has written down how he knows.

  3. Auntie Fola

    Nine Hundred Loaves

    A film about Auntie Fola·14 min·Oct 2024

    Every morning since 1998, Auntie Fola has baked the same nine hundred loaves in the same order, starting with the ones that need the longest to prove. The film covers one shift, from 02:15 to opening time.

  4. Viktor

    The Man Who Repairs the Signal

    A film about Viktor·26 min·Jun 2024

    Viktor maintains eleven relay stations on a mountain range, alone, in a van that is older than he is. The film is about the work and about the part of it he does not describe as work at all.

  5. Chidera

    A Room with Two Windows

    A film about Chidera·12 min·Nov 2023

    Chidera has lived in four countries and keeps one box unpacked in every one of them. A twelve-minute film about the specific objects a person chooses to carry, filmed entirely in a single room.
