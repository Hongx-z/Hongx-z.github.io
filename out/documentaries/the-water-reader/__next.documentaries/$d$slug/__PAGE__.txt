3:T510,<p>The first thing Idris said to me was that he was not a scientist. The second thing he said was that the scientists call him twice a year.</p>
<p>He walks a route of eleven pumps and listens to each one for between thirty seconds and two minutes. From that he produces a number — the depth of the water table, to within about a hand's width. The catchment authority has a logger at four of the eleven sites. He has been right about the other seven, and about the four, more often than the loggers have.</p>
<h2 id="directors-note"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#directors-note">Director's note</a></h2>
<p>This is a film about a skill that is about to stop existing, and I wanted to avoid the two obvious traps: making it a lament, and making it a mystery.</p>
<p>So the film does not ask <em>how does he know</em>. It asks what his day is like, and how long the walk is, and what he does when he is wrong — which happened once during the shoot, and which is the reason this film exists in the form it does.</p>
